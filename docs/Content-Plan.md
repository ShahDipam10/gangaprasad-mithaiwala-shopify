# Content Plan (Phase 1 deliverable)

Fictional brand facts, sitemap, and the full mock product catalog used to
populate the theme in Phase 5. This is the source of truth for copy —
update here first, then propagate into the theme.

## Brand facts

- **Name**: Gangaprasad Mithaiwala
- **Founded**: 1919, Anand, Gujarat — currently run by the third
  generation (grandson of the founder), same family recipes throughout.
- **Signature product**: Kesar Doodh Peda — a nod to Anand's identity as
  India's dairy capital (home of Amul).
- **Differentiator**: fresh cow's milk sourced daily from local Anand dairy
  cooperatives, no preservatives, small daily batches rather than
  mass-produced stock — positioned against more generic/mass-market
  competitors like Candy Envy.
- **Voice**: warm, heritage-forward, proud but not boastful — "three
  generations, one recipe" rather than corporate marketing speak.

## FAQ topics

Shelf life/freshness window per category, shipping timelines + COD
availability, damaged-in-transit returns, bulk/corporate gifting orders,
allergen info (dairy, nuts, gluten present across the catalog).

## Policy pages

items damaged in transit**, since that's the realistic policy for a food
