# Sweet Shop Shopify Store – Project Brief

Store: **Gangaprasad Mithaiwala** — a century-old Gujarati mithai (sweet)
manufacturer known for laddus, barfi, gulab jamun, and savory namkeen,
based in Anand, Gujarat with a 100+ year legacy. Sweets in Indian culture
are cherished symbols of celebration, exchanged during festivals, weddings,
and special occasions — the site should celebrate this heritage with warm,
festive visuals and language (e.g. "100+ years of tradition").

## Inspiration & Example Stores

- **Ajwaah Sweets (Shopify)** — strong "About Us" heritage story, clean
  layout, high-quality photos, trust badges (payment icons, social links).
- **Candy Envy (Shopify)** — rich categorization ("Shop by Color/Theme/
  Occasion"), promotional banners, clear CTAs, "Why Buy From Us" trust
  section.
- **Mithaiwala NYC (Squarespace)** — cultural storytelling block explaining
  Indian sweets culture and festive gatherings.
- **Shopify "Sweet" theme** — reference feature set: high-res image
  galleries, product image zoom, promotional banners, FAQ/contact sections,
  cross-selling widgets, trust badges. (We're building on the free **Dawn**
  theme instead and replicating the relevant features.)

## Design & Cultural Aesthetic

- **Color & visuals** — India's vibrant festive palette: saffron orange,
  maroon/red, gold accents, jade green. Traditional patterns (paisleys,
  floral/mango motifs, rangoli). Imagery of iconic sweets (laddoos, jalebi
  spirals, kaju katli). Hero banners could evoke Diwali (diyas, marigold
  garlands).
- **Imagery** — high-quality, appetizing photos; lifestyle shots conveying
  togetherness/tradition; descriptive alt-text for SEO + accessibility.
- **Typography** — elegant but readable; a classic serif or
  Devanagari-inspired display font paired with a clean body font.
- **Layout & UX** — key promotions/bestsellers above the fold, strong-contrast
  primary CTAs (e.g. "Shop Now"), simple flattened navigation (major
  collections in header, utility links in footer), fully responsive/mobile
  (60%+ of traffic expected on mobile).

## Essential Pages & Features

- **Home** — hero banner + CTA, featured categories, bestsellers, brand
  tagline/story blurb, current offers, reviews/press if available.
- **About Us** — Gangaprasad's 100+ year legacy in Anand, Gujarat; artisanal
  process/family tradition; shop/team photos for authenticity.
- **Collections** — e.g. "Sweets (Mithai)", "Namkeen (Savory Snacks)",
  "Festival Specials"; filtering/sorting; SEO intro paragraph per category.
- **Product Detail Pages** — title, image gallery w/ zoom, price, variants
  (weight/size), Add-to-Cart above the fold, ingredients/shelf-life/flavor
  notes, reviews, trust badges, related-products cross-sell.
- **Contact** — contact form, address/phone/email, Google Map embed, store
  hours, WhatsApp/live-chat link, social icons.
- **FAQ** — shipping, freshness, returns.
- **Blog/News (optional)** — SEO/storytelling (e.g. "Top Diwali Mithai
  Recipes").
- **Policies** — Privacy, Terms of Service, Return/Refund, Shipping Info.
- **Navigation** — flattened header menu, sticky cart icon, search bar.

## Technical Integrations

- **Platform** — Shopify store (Basic plan or higher), Dawn theme
  customized with branding via sections/blocks.
- **Products** — title, images, price, description, SEO tags, variants for
  size/weight, alt text on every image.
- **Payments** — India-preferred methods via third-party gateways
  (Razorpay, Cashfree, etc.) since Shopify Payments isn't available in
  India: card, UPI, net banking, wallets, Cash-on-Delivery (COD expected by
  most Indian buyers), plus PayPal for international/card orders.
- **Shipping** — India shipping app (Shiprocket/Shipway) for real-time
  rates, label printing, courier tracking; delivery zones; estimated
  delivery date on PDP (important for perishables).
- **Taxes/legal** — GST configuration; clear tax-inclusive/exclusive
  pricing; checkout email/SMS notifications.
- **Marketing** — SEO (titles/meta/URLs), newsletter capture (Shopify
  Email/Klaviyo) with signup incentive, upsell/cross-sell + festive
  bundles, social proof (Instagram feed/reviews), abandoned cart recovery.
- **Security/perf** — HTTPS by default, optimized images, minimal
  heavy scripts, mobile-first.

## Project Task Sequence (from the original brief)

1. Wireframe & design each page (Home, Collection, Product, About/Contact).
2. Shopify setup: create store, pick/install theme, configure nav/footer.
3. Populate content: products with images and SEO-friendly descriptions,
   About page brand history, logo/site colors.
4. Customizations: collection filtering/sorting, trust badges, newsletter
   block, at least one blog post.
5. Integrations: payment gateway apps, shipping app + courier accounts,
   reviews app, Google Analytics + Facebook Pixel.
6. SEO & testing: alt-text/meta tags, checkout flow test per payment method
   (especially COD/UPI), mobile responsiveness, performance, legal pages.
7. Launch prep: pre-launch checklist (backup theme, test order), staff
   training, marketing plan.
