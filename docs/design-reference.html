Our story

Three generations, one recipe

Gangaprasad Mithaiwala opened its doors in 1919 in Anand, Gujarat — a town that would later become India's dairy capital. That timing was no accident: our founder built the business on fresh cow's milk from local cooperatives, and that's still exactly where every peda and barfi starts today.

Three generations later, the recipes haven't changed. What has changed is very little — we still make small batches daily rather than stockpiling, because that's the only way the sweets taste the way they did in 1919.

A century, briefly

Our timeline

1919

Founded in Anand, Gujarat, sourcing fresh milk from local dairy farmers.

1960s

Anand becomes India's dairy capital — our milk sourcing scales with the cooperative movement.

Today

Third generation at the helm, same recipes, now bringing the shop online.

Frequently asked questions

How long do the sweets stay fresh?

Milk-based sweets like peda and barfi are best within 4 days; dry sweets like soan papdi keep for 2–3 weeks. Refrigerate on arrival for milk sweets.

Do you ship pan-India, and is COD available?

Yes — we ship across India with 2–4 day delivery, and cash on delivery is available in most pincodes.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Contact us within 24 hours with a photo and we'll replace it or refund it in full — see our refund policy for details.

Can I order in bulk for a wedding or corporate event?

Yes — reach out via our contact page at least a week ahead for bulk and custom gifting orders.

Do your sweets contain nuts, dairy or gluten?

Most products contain dairy; several contain nuts or gluten. Full allergen details are listed on each product page.

Refund policy

Privacy policy Terms of service Refund policy Shipping policy

Because our sweets and namkeen are perishable, we can't accept returns once an order has been delivered.

If your order arrives damaged, spoiled, or incorrect, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with a photo of the item. We'll issue a full replacement or refund — whichever you prefer.

Refunds are credited to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. For cash-on-delivery orders, refunds are issued as store credit or a direct bank transfer.

Bulk and custom gifting orders (weddings, corporate hampers) follow a separate cancellation window, confirmed at the time of order.
